## Design Context

### Users
VibeHUD is for macOS developers who use Claude Code and want to monitor sessions, handle approvals, and reply without leaving their current workflow.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, and premium. The product should feel like a native macOS system surface: technically capable without becoming loud, playful, or ornamental.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a restrained dark palette with cool-tinted graphite surfaces, fine silver details, and purposeful transparent glass. The primary reference is a compact terminal-style app icon with a dark rounded base, a glass upper chamber, and a minimal `>_` prompt. Avoid neon developer tropes, colorful gradients, generic AI glow, and glass effects applied without functional hierarchy.

### Design Principles
- Make the terminal prompt the immediate, unmistakable brand mark.
- Use glass only to establish a meaningful upper surface or layer.
- Preserve strong silhouettes and contrast at small macOS icon sizes.
- Prefer cool graphite, smoked glass, and silver over pure black and white.
- Keep visual depth subtle, physical, and consistent with native macOS materials.
